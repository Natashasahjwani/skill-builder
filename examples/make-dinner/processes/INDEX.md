# Make Dinner — Process Index

Each process is one style of meal, with its own shopping list, timeline, and steps. `strategy.md` picks one; this is the menu it picks from. Load `SKILL.md` first; every process assumes its hard rules.

- **taco-bar**: everyone builds their own plate from a spread of toppings. Best for mixed diets, groups of four to ten, and forty-five minutes or more.
- **one-pot-pasta**: pasta cooked in the sauce in a single pot. Best for under thirty minutes, two to five people, and a kitchen with pasta and one good thing to put in it.
- **sheet-pan-roast**: a protein and vegetables roasted together on trays. Best for six or more people, an hour or more, and a cook who wants hands-off time while it is in the oven.
