# Process — Taco Bar

Everyone builds their own plate from a spread of fillings and toppings. The cook makes the parts; the guests make the meals. This is why it handles mixed diets without a second dish.

## When to pick

- Best for: a vegetarian or a strong dislike at the table, groups of four to ten, forty-five minutes or more.
- Worst for: under thirty minutes, a kitchen with no tortillas or wraps and no way to get them, a table too small for the spread.

## Required inputs

- Tortillas or something to wrap in.
- Two fillings, one with meat and one without. Beans count.
- Three or more toppings from what is on hand.
- The number of people, to size the fillings.

## Execution

1. **Set the serving time** and count back. The fillings take twenty-five minutes. Toppings take ten. Warming tortillas takes five. Start the fillings forty minutes before serving.

2. **Start the two fillings** in separate pans. Season them the same way so the plates taste like one meal. The vegetarian filling is a real filling, not the meat one with the meat left out.

3. **Prep the toppings while the fillings cook.** Chop, grate, and slice into small bowls. Anything that browns when cut, cut last.

4. **Warm the tortillas** in the last five minutes, wrapped in a towel, in a low oven or a dry pan.

5. **Lay it out** in the order a plate gets built: wraps, fillings, toppings, sauces. Guests move down the line.

## Output content

- The shopping list for the two fillings and the toppings that are missing.
- The timeline from forty minutes out.
- The steps above with the cue for each filling being done.
- The layout order for the spread.

## What never to do

- Serve one filling and call the meat-free option "just skip the meat."
- Cut the toppings that brown before the fillings are on.
- Put the spread out before the tortillas are warm. Cold wraps end a taco bar early.
